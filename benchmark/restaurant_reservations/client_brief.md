Client: the owner of a chop bar in Cape Coast.

- Tables have a table number and a number of seats. They want to see the list of tables.
- A reservation is for one table and has the customer's name, phone number, party size, date and time (a start time like 19:30). No accounts.
- A party bigger than the table's seats is refused; a party exactly equal to the seats is fine.
- The same table cannot be reserved twice for the same date and time. Another table at that time, or the same table at another time or date, is fine.
- Reserving a table that does not exist is refused.
- They want to see every reservation on a given date, across all tables.
- Cancelling or editing reservations and joining tables are not needed now.
