Our campus has a lost-and-found desk and we want a simple board for it. Anyone who finds something can report it with a short description of the item, where they found it and the date they found it. People should be able to see all the items on the board and look at one item. When the owner comes to collect an item, the desk marks it as claimed and writes down the owner's name. An item that has already been claimed cannot be claimed again. We also want to see only the items that are still waiting for their owners.
