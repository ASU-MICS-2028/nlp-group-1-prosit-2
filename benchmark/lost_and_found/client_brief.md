Client: the staff member who runs the lost-and-found desk at a university campus near Accra.

- A found item has a short description, the place it was found and the date it was found.
- Anyone can report an item; no accounts.
- An item starts unclaimed. Claiming it records the owner's name and marks it claimed. An owner's name is required to claim.
- A claimed item cannot be claimed again; the first owner's name stays.
- They need to see all items, one item, and only the items still waiting to be claimed.
- No photos, editing or deleting for now.
