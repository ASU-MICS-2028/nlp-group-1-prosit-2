Client: the events coordinator of a church in Accra.

- Events have a name, a date and a maximum number of people.
- Anyone can sign up with a name and a phone number; no accounts.
- Each event keeps its own list of sign-ups. The same person may sign up for several events.
- Once an event has as many sign-ups as its maximum, further sign-ups are refused.
- They need to see all events, one event, and who signed up for an event.
- Editing or deleting events is not needed now.
