Client: the manager of a private student hostel near the university in Legon.

- A room has a name and a number of beds (1 or more).
- A booking is for a room (not a specific bed): student name, arrival date, leaving date (YYYY-MM-DD). Leaving must be after arrival.
- A booking occupies a bed on each night from arrival up to the night before leaving. Leaving on the 12th and arriving on the 12th do not clash.
- A booking is refused if on any night of the stay the room already has as many bookings as beds.
- A student can cancel; the bed is then free for those nights. Cancelling twice is refused. A cancelled booking cannot be restored.
- "Free beds for a stay" per room = beds minus the bookings on the busiest night of that stay.
- No payments for now.
