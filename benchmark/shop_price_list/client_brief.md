Client: the owner of a small provisions shop in Madina, Accra.

- Each item has a name, a price in Ghana cedis (pesewas allowed, e.g. 12.50) and a category the owner chooses (e.g. Drinks, Toiletries, Foodstuff).
- Every price must be more than zero; 0 or negative prices are refused, both when adding and when changing a price.
- Prices change often, so the owner must be able to change the price of an item.
- Two items cannot have the same name; names are compared ignoring upper/lower case ("Milo" and "MILO" are the same). A second item with the same name is refused.
- The owner wants to see all items, or only the items in one category, and look up one item.
- Removing an item takes it off the list completely: it is no longer listed and looking it up says not found.
- No stock counts, sales or receipts for now.
