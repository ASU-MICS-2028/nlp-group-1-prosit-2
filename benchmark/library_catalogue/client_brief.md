Client: the librarian of a junior high school in Cape Coast.

- A book has a title, an author and the number of copies the library owns.
- Each book shows how many copies are still on the shelf; a new book has all its copies on the shelf.
- A student borrows one copy by giving their name; this takes one copy off the shelf.
- When no copies are on the shelf, borrowing is refused until a copy is returned.
- A student returns a copy by giving their name; this puts the copy back. A student who has not borrowed that book cannot return it.
- Each book keeps its own count; borrowing one book does not change another.
- No accounts, due dates, fines, editing or removing books for now.
