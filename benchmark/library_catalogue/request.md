Our school library wants a simple catalogue of its books. The librarian should be able to add a book with its title, its author and how many copies the library owns. Students should be able to see all the books, and look at one book to see how many copies are still on the shelf. A student borrows a book by giving their name, and returns it when they are done; a student can only return a book they actually borrowed. When every copy of a book is out, nobody else can borrow it until a copy comes back.
