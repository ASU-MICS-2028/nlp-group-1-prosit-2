Client: a student who shares trip, food and rent costs with friends in Accra.

- A group has a name and members (by name).
- An expense: who paid (a member), the amount in cedis (more than 0, at most 2 decimals) and which members share it (at least one; the payer need not be among them).
- The amount is split equally among the chosen members. If it does not divide evenly, shares differ by at most one pesewa and add up exactly to the amount; who gets the extra pesewa does not matter.
- Each member's balance = paid for expenses - own shares + paid back to others - received back from others. Positive = owed money, negative = owes. Balances in a group always sum to zero.
- Settling up: one member pays another back an amount (more than 0); the payer's balance rises and the receiver's falls by that amount. Paying yourself is refused. Overpaying is allowed.
- Everyone named in an expense or payment must be a member of that group.
