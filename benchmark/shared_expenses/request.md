My friends and I share costs for trips, food and rent, and we want something that keeps track of who owes whom. One of us creates a group and adds the friends to it by name. When someone pays for something, we record who paid, how much in cedis, and which members of the group it is shared between; it is split equally between those members, and the person who paid does not have to be one of them. If a bill does not split evenly, the shares may differ by at most one pesewa, and together they must add up exactly to the bill. For every member we want to see their balance, which is how much they are owed overall, or how much they owe if it is negative, and the balances in a group must always add up to zero. When one friend pays another back, we record that payment and the balances change to match. Amounts must be more than zero, everyone involved must be in the group, and nobody can pay themselves back.
