I run a small tailoring shop in Kumasi and I want to keep my orders in order. I need to save each customer with their name, phone number and their measurements: chest, waist, hip and length, all in inches. For a customer I take orders, and each order has the garment, the date it is due, the price and the deposit the customer has paid, in cedis. The deposit can never be more than the price. I want to see all the orders for one customer and look at a single order. I also want to see every order that is due on or before a date I choose, so I know what to finish first. When a customer picks up their clothes I mark the order as collected, and collected orders should no longer show in that due list.
