Client: a tailor who runs a small shop in Kumasi.

- Customers have a name, a phone number and measurements (chest, waist, hip, length, in inches).
- Each customer can have many orders. An order has a garment, a due date, a price and a deposit, both in cedis.
- A deposit larger than the price is refused. A deposit equal to the price (fully paid) is fine.
- They want to see all orders for one customer (collected or not) and look at one order.
- The due list shows orders due on or before the chosen date, including that day, and leaves out collected orders.
- Marking an order collected is permanent; there is no undo.
- Editing or deleting customers and orders is not needed now.
