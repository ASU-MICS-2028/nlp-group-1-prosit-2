Client: the organiser of a community football league in Kumasi.

- Teams have a name.
- A fixture has a home team, an away team and a date. Both teams must exist and must be different.
- A fixture has no score until one is recorded. A score is the number of goals for the home side and for the away side, each 0 or more; negative numbers are refused. Recording again replaces the old score.
- They need to see one fixture, all teams, and all fixtures of one team (home and away).
- No league table, points, players or editing teams for now.
