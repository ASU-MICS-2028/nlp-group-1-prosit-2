I run a community football league in Kumasi and I need to keep track of our fixtures. First we add the teams, each with its name. Then we schedule a fixture on a date between a home team and an away team, and of course a team cannot play against itself. After the match we record the score, meaning how many goals each side scored, and a score can never be negative. We also want to see all the fixtures of one team, whether that team plays at home or away.
