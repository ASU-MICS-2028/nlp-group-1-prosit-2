We want a simple mobile money wallet service for the customers of our shop in Accra. A customer opens a wallet with their name and phone number, and one phone number can only have one wallet. Customers can put money into their own wallet and send money from their wallet to someone else's wallet. Amounts are in Ghana cedis and pesewas, so never more than two decimal places, and every amount must be more than zero. Nobody can send more than they have, so a wallet's balance must never go below zero, and sending money to your own wallet is not allowed. The money must add up to the exact pesewa every time, with no rounding mistakes. We want to check any wallet's balance and see its history of every deposit and transfer, showing the amount and what the balance was right after each one.
