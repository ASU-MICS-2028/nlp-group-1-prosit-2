Client: the front-desk manager of a small clinic in Tamale.

- Doctors have a name and a specialty. Staff need to see the list of doctors.
- An appointment has a doctor, the patient's name (no patient accounts), a date and a time slot written as a start time like 09:00 or 14:30.
- The same doctor cannot be booked twice on the same date at the same time; the second booking is refused. Another doctor at the same time is fine, and the same doctor at another time or date is fine.
- Booking with a doctor who does not exist is refused.
- They view one doctor's appointments for one date at a time.
- Cancelling removes the appointment entirely and frees the slot. Cancelling an appointment that does not exist is refused.
- Rescheduling and editing doctors are not needed now.
