We run a small clinic in Tamale with a few doctors, and we want patients to book appointments. We need to add each doctor with their name and what they specialise in, and see the list of doctors. A patient books with a doctor by giving their name, the date and the time slot, for example 09:00 or 14:30. A doctor cannot have two appointments on the same date in the same time slot, so a second booking for that slot must be refused, but a different doctor can still be booked for it. We want to see all of one doctor's appointments for a particular day. If a patient cancels, the appointment is removed and the slot becomes free for someone else to book.
