Client: a provisions seller with a stall at Makola market, Accra.

- Each item has a name, a current quantity (whole number) and a low level (whole number).
- A sale lowers the quantity. Selling exactly the remaining quantity is allowed (it goes to zero); selling more than is in stock is refused and nothing changes.
- A restock raises the quantity.
- Sale and restock amounts must be more than zero; zero or negative is refused.
- An item is "running low" when its quantity is at or below its low level, including exactly at it.
- They want to see all items, one item, and the running-low list. No prices, buyers or history needed now.
