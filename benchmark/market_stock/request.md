I sell provisions at Makola market and I want to keep track of my stock. For each item I want to save its name, how many I have, and the level at which I consider it to be running low. When I sell some of an item the quantity should go down, but I must not be able to sell more than I have. When new goods arrive I record a restock and the quantity goes up. The amount sold or restocked must always be more than zero. I want to see all my items, look at one item, and see a list of the items that are at or below their low level so I know what to buy.
