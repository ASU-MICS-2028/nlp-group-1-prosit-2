Client: the collector who runs a market susu group in Kumasi.

- A group has a name and members; each member has a name and a phone number.
- Contributions are recorded per member per round (round 1, 2, 3, ...), in cedis with at most 2 decimal places, always more than zero. A member may pay more than once in a round; it all counts.
- Members do not have to pay the same amount.
- At the end of a round one member collects the pot: exactly the total of all contributions for that round in that group, with no commission.
- One collector per round. A member collects at most once per cycle; one group is one cycle, and the next cycle is a new group.
- A round with nothing paid in cannot be collected. Once collected, a round is closed to further contributions.
- A member's statement shows the total they paid in and the total they received.
- They need to see the members and the payouts made so far. No editing or deleting for now.
