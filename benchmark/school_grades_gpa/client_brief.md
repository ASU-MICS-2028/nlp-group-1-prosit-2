Client: the exams officer of a small college in Cape Coast.

- Students have a name. Courses have a name and credit hours: a whole number from 1 to 6; anything else is refused.
- Lecturers enter a score for a student in a course: a whole number from 0 to 100; anything outside is refused. Entering a score again for the same student and course replaces the old one (only the latest counts, the course appears once in the results).
- Grading scale (score -> letter, grade point):
  - 80-100 A 4.0
  - 75-79 B+ 3.5
  - 70-74 B 3.0
  - 65-69 C+ 2.5
  - 60-64 C 2.0
  - 55-59 D+ 1.5
  - 50-54 D 1.0
  - 0-49 F 0.0
- GPA is weighted by credit hours: sum of (grade point x credit hours) / sum of credit hours, over every course the student has a score in, F included. Rounded to 2 decimal places (normal rounding). A student with no scores shows 0.
- The results show each course the student has a score in with the score, letter and grade point, plus the GPA.
