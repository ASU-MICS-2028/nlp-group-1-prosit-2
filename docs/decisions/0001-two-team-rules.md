# 0001. Two team rules: explain simply, test every alternative

- Status: Accepted
- Date: 2026-10-18
- Decided by: Joseph

## Context

Each person owns one part of the platform and nobody else will redo or read it. The report's Section B, the presentation and every member's viva depend on understanding each part. The rubric rewards justified choices and real results.

## Options considered

- Let each person document however they like and choose without comparing alternatives.
- Two firm rules for everyone.

## Decision

1. **Explain everything as blatantly and simply as possible.** Everything a person builds is explained in their `learnings/` file for a teammate who has never seen the code.
2. **Test every alternative.** No decision is made without comparing at least two options, measured the same way, with the numbers logged, including the options that lost.

## Consequences

- Every workstream keeps an experiment log; losing options are kept as results.
- More work per decision, but Section B can justify every choice with numbers.
