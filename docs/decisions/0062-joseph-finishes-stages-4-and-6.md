## Context

On Nov 12 every stage except 4 (Michael, UI and usability) and 6 (Eric, integration and writing) was done. The team aimed to finish the week of Nov 9. The open tasks were S4.1, S4.3 to S4.6 and S6.2 to S6.5. Bernard's open work had already moved to Joseph the same way (S4.7, decision 0058).

## Options considered

- Wait for Michael and Eric: their parts stay theirs, but the submission slips past the week we aimed for.
- Joseph takes over the tasks that need only the repo and the server (page changes, comparisons, Section B, slides, the demo recording, the learnings files), and leaves what only people can do.

## Consequences

The plan can finish without waiting. Michael and Eric still present and defend their parts (talk slots in `docs/PLAN.md`), so they must read the W3 and W5 learnings files before the viva; the gaps only Michael can fill (his design reasons from before Nov 6) are marked in his file. Until S4.5 runs, Section B 4.5 has no usability numbers, only the word count (W3 rows 4 and 7).
