# The course: ICS554 Natural Language Processing

Summarized from the 2026 course outline. MSc/MPhil, CSIS Department, Ashesi University, Semester 1. Instructor: Dennis Owusu. Co-instructor: Frank Acquaye. Classes on Tuesday afternoons and Thursday afternoons.

## What the course covers

Statistical and neural language models, LLMs, LLM applications and agentic systems, and speech recognition, with a focus on African languages. Reading and implementing research papers is a large part of it.

Topics: n-gram language models · LLMs and pre-training · LLM post-training (instruction tuning, alignment, test-time compute) · information retrieval · retrieval augmented generation · agentic systems · speech recognition · speech recognition in low-resource settings · fine-tuning speech recognition models.

## How it is taught

- **Prosits** (problem situations): 3 in the semester. The group meets to understand the problem and plan ("time with group"), each member works on their part ("autonomy"), then the group meets again to compare, debate and combine the work into one submission.
- **Workshops**: lectures, labs and Q&A sessions. Some are student-led: bring questions from your prosit work.

80–100 is an A. No make-ups except for university-accepted reasons such as illness.

## AI policy

AI use is allowed and encouraged for learning and solving problems, but not mindlessly. The test is: **"Can I explain and defend everything I have submitted, without AI, when asked?"** In-class assessments (vivas, quizzes) carry a large share of the grade, and failing to answer questions about your own submission lowers it significantly.
