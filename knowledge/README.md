## Adding to the repo

- **Transcripts are never committed raw.** Lecture, meeting or call recordings get summarized first: lecture summaries go in `knowledge/lectures/lecture-NN.md`, meeting summaries go in `docs/meetings/YYYY-MM-DD.md`. Follow the format of the existing files.
- **A summary must only say what was actually said.** Mark anything unclear as unclear. Never fill gaps from memory.
- **The repo is public.** Leave out student names and IDs, phone numbers, emails and anything personal. Say "the instructor" or "a student".
- **Put knowledge where people will look for it.** About the project in general → here. What you found in your own workstream → `learnings/`. A decision → a new record in `docs/decisions/`.
